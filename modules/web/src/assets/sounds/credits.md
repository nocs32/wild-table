# Sounds

The game's sounds come from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under **CC0** (public domain), so no credit is required. They're credited here anyway.

| File | Source | Author | What was done to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chime.wav` | [Bell Chime Alert](https://freesound.org/people/plasterbrain/sounds/419493/) | plasterbrain | left channel only (the two channels partly cancel when mixed to mono), silence trimmed from the start, the cut-off tail faded out over 0.2 s, evened out in level |

All of them are mono, 16-bit, 48 kHz, cut from Freesound's high-quality previews.
